Hoziana privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Hoziana is the hymnal of Korali Hoziyana, ADEPR Paruwasi ya Nyarugenge, on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it does not collect personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your favourite hymns, your playlists, recently opened hymns and your settings (such as text size and dark mode) are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Hymn updates

When you open the app with an internet connection, it downloads any corrections or new hymns the choir has published. This request does not include your name, contacts, location or any identifier for you or your phone. Like any website, the servers that deliver the hymns (Supabase, our hosting provider) may briefly record technical details such as your IP address in their normal security logs. We do not use these to identify you, and we do not sell or share them.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the app hands the hymn text to your phone's own share menu. What happens next depends on the app you choose to share with.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to sign up or sign in.
  • It does not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It does not sell or share information about you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
